What it is

Log a set in a few taps.

Haapa is for anyone who finds the gym a bit much, from your first day to your fifth year. Big buttons, one hand, and nothing that shouts.

  • All as planned, in one tap. Your planned weight and reps are already filled in. Tap only what went differently.
  • Train first, log later. Log live at the gym, or report back afterwards: today, yesterday, or any past day, with or without a routine.
  • Rest starts by itself when you finish a set, with −15 and +15 seconds.
  • See how you're doing. Estimated 1RM, best sets, and a quiet PR tag when you beat a record.
  • A plate calculator that tells you what to put on each side of the bar.

The progression grid

Know what you'll lift next week.

Exercises in rows, weeks in columns. Enter week 1, pick your rule, and Haapa fills in the weeks after it.

  • Pick your own rule: add 2.5 kg a week, add a rep, or climb a rep range.
  • Sets at more than one weight. Top sets, back-off sets, or a pyramid, all following the same rule.
  • A week is your next workout. Miss a week at the gym and you pick up where you left off.
  • Change any cell by hand. Your edits stay, and later weeks follow them.
  • Share your plan. Send a whole program as a QR code or a file, or start one in Excel. For coaches and personal trainers, or just between friends.

Timers

Timers in the same app.

Rest between sets, HIIT, and your own intervals, like "Burpees 40 s, rest 20 s, plank 60 s". Use them in a workout or on their own.

  • Ready-made or your own. Start from Tabata or every minute on the minute, or build a timer from parts and save it.
  • Change parts remind you to switch sides or weights.
  • Stretching when you're done. 52 stretches, each with its own timer, at your own pace. The time is added to your workout.
  • Works with the screen locked. Beeps, gongs, vibration, and a 3-2-1 before every change.
