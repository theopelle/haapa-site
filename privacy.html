Privacy

The short version: Haapa collects nothing. Your workouts stay on your phone.

Last updated 14 November 2026.

What the app collects

Nothing. Haapa has no account, no analytics, no crash reports, no ads, and no tracking of any kind. The app never goes online: on Android it doesn't even ask for internet access. The App Store and Google Play listings say "Data not collected".

Where your data lives

Your routines, plans, workouts, timers, and settings are stored only on your phone, inside the app. No one else has a copy, including us. That also means we can't recover your data for you. If you delete the app or lose your phone, the data goes with it, unless it's in a backup.

Your phone's own backup (Google on Android, iCloud or your computer on iPhone) includes Haapa's data when it's turned on. That backup is between you and Google or Apple, under your own account. We never see it.

You can also make a backup file yourself: in Settings, under Your data, tap Back up now. You choose where the file goes, for example your own cloud drive or your computer. The guide shows both ways.

Files you share

  • Backups and CSV exports are files you save or send yourself, through your phone's share sheet. Where they go is up to you.
  • Sharing a plan with a QR code or a file goes straight from your phone to the other person. Nothing passes through us. The QR code is read by the other phone's own camera, and Haapa never asks for camera access.
  • Your name, if you give one, is only used for the greeting on the Home screen. It stays on your phone and isn't included in shared plans.

Notifications

Timers use notifications so you hear the cues with the screen locked. They're scheduled by the app on your phone. Nothing is sent from a server.

Reporting a bug

Report a bug opens your own email app with a message to hello@haapa.app. Copy app info puts the app version, your phone's system version, and simple counts (for example, how many workouts you have) on your clipboard, so you can paste them if you want to. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself. We only use your email to answer you.

This website

haapa.app uses no cookies, no analytics, and no tracking, and its fonts and images are hosted here, with no requests to other companies. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep short-term server logs, such as IP addresses, for security. We don't see or use them.

Hearing when Haapa is out

If you email hello@haapa.app to hear when Haapa is out, we keep your address only to send that one email, then delete it. We don't share it with anyone.
