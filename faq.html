Questions about Haapa

Short answers to what people ask most. Something missing? Write to hello@haapa.app.

Common questions

When can I get Haapa?

In early 2027, on iPhone and Android at the same time. Tap “Tell me when it's out” on the home page, and we'll send you one email when it's ready.

Is it really free? What's the catch?

There's no catch. No ads, no subscription, no paid features, and no in-app purchases. Haapa costs very little to run, and it's built on one idea: an app should serve you, not ask you for money.

Why isn't there a premium version?

Because a training log should work fully for everyone, not stop at four routines or three months of history. With Haapa, everyone gets the whole app: nothing is locked, there's no trial, and nothing to unlock later. If Haapa helps you train, tell a friend, or leave a review on the App Store or Google Play. That's all we ask.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. Open the app and start. Haapa works fully offline on iPhone and Android, and it never sends anything over the internet.

Where is my data, and how do I keep it safe?

Only on your phone, never on our servers (we have none). There are two ways to keep it safe:

  • Automatic: your phone's own backup (Google on Android, iCloud or your computer on iPhone) includes Haapa. Set up a new phone from that backup, and your workouts come back.
  • By hand: Back up now in Settings makes one file that you can save anywhere. Restore it on a new phone, or merge two phones into one.

The step-by-step guide shows both.

Can I bring my history from Strong or Hevy?

Yes. Export a CSV file from Strong or Hevy and import it into Haapa. You see a preview first, and you can match exercise names before anything is saved.

Does it work with Excel or Google Sheets?

Yes. Export every set as a CSV file, or start a plan from Haapa's Excel template, in English and Finnish. The columns are described on the CSV format page.

Will Haapa write a training program for me?

No. You choose the exercises, the starting weights, and the rule, like +2.5 kg a week. Haapa applies your rule and keeps track. If you have a coach, they can send you their plan as a file or a QR code.

What happens if I miss a workout or a target?

A week in Haapa is your next workout of that routine, not a calendar week, so a missed week at the gym doesn't skip a step. If you miss a target, you choose: repeat the week, or go on.

Can I log after the workout instead of during it?

Yes. Report back for today, yesterday, or any past day, with or without a routine. Tap “All as planned”, or change only what was different.

Do the timers work with the screen locked?

Yes. Beeps, gongs, vibration, and a 3-2-1 countdown keep coming when your phone is locked or in your pocket.

Can I share a plan with a client or a friend?

Yes. Share a whole routine as a QR code or a file. The other person scans the code with their phone's camera, or opens the file, and the plan appears in their Haapa.

Can I use pounds?

Not yet. Haapa uses kilograms for now. Pounds will come if enough people ask for them.

Which phones does it run on?

iPhones with iOS 15 or later, and Android phones with Android 7 or later. There's no iPad or Apple Watch version.

Which languages does it support?

English and Finnish, including every exercise name. More languages may follow.

How do I report a bug or suggest an idea?

Write to hello@haapa.app, or use “Report a bug” or “Suggest an exercise or a feature” in the app's Settings. Your mail app opens, and nothing is sent unless you send it.

Why is it called Haapa?

Haapa is Finnish for aspen. Its pale, soft wood is a classic for sauna benches, because it stays comfortable to sit on even when the sauna is hot. Aspen leaves tremble in the lightest breeze, and the icon is one of them, with a lake and a low sun inside. Calm, warm, and a little Finnish, like the app.
